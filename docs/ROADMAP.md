## Project: cloud-migrator
**Status:** In Development
**Context:** Axentx Autonomous AI-Workforce Pipeline

---

## Overview
`cloud-migrator` is an AI-driven platform designed to bridge the gap between high-level business objectives and concrete technical cloud implementations. By leveraging Axentx's high-throughput inference engines and validated datasets, the tool will translate natural language migration goals into executable infrastructure-as-code (IaC) strategies.

---

## MVP Milestone (Launch Critical)
**Goal:** Deliver a shippable "Translator" that converts business intent into a structured migration plan
