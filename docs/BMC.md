# Business Model Canvas – cloud‑migrator

| **Section** | **Details** |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Value Proposition** | • **End‑to‑end migration planning** – From strategy to execution, a single tool that maps business goals to cloud architecture.<br>• **Risk‑aware roadmaps** – Automated risk assessment, cost forecasting, and compliance checks.<br>• **Automated execution** – Generate IaC templates (Terraform, Pulumi) and orchestrate lift‑and‑shift or re‑architect workflows.<br>• **Continuous validation** – Real‑time monitoring of performance, cost, and security post‑migration.<br>• **Collaboration hub** – Role‑based dashboards, task assignment, and audit trails for PMs, architects, and ops teams. |
| **Customer Segments** | 1. **Large enterprises (10k+ employees)** looking to modernize legacy workloads.<br>2. **Mid‑market SaaS companies** migrating to multi‑cloud or hybrid environments.<br>3. **Managed service providers (MSPs)** offering migration as a service.<br>4. **Public sector agencies** with strict compliance requirements. |
| **Channels** | • **Direct sales** – Dedicated account managers for enterprise accounts.<br>• **Partner ecosystem** – Integrations with major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) and consulting firms.<br>• **Online marketplace** – SaaS listing on AWS Marketplace, Azure Marketplace, and Google Cloud Marketplace.<br>• **Content marketing** – Webinars, case studies, and technical blogs showcasing migration success stories. |
| **Revenue Streams** | • **Subscription (SaaS)** – Tiered plans (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) based on user seats and workload size.<br>• **Professional services** – Migration strategy workshops, custom integrations, and training.<br>• **Marketplace fees** – Revenue share from cloud provider marketplace listings.<br>• **Data‑driven insights** – Optional analytics add‑on for cost optimization and performance benchmarking. |
| **Cost Structure** | • **Cloud hosting & storage** – SaaS infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and data lake.<br>• **R&D** – Feature development, AI/ML model training, and security compliance.<br>• **Sales & marketing** – Account executives, partner enablement, and content creation.<br>• **Support & SLAs** – 24/7 helpdesk, incident response, and uptime guarantees.<br>• **Compliance & audit** – Certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2, FedRAMP). |
| **Key Resources** | • **Core product team** – Engineers, product managers, UX designers.<br>• **AI/ML models** – For risk scoring, cost forecasting, and automated IaC generation.<br>• **Knowledge base** – Repository of migration patterns, best practices, and compliance checklists.<br>• **Partner APIs** – Integrations with cloud provider SDKs and third‑party tools.<br>• **Customer success org** – Onboarding, training, and community forums. |
| **Key Activities** | • **Product development** – Building migration planners, IaC generators, and monitoring dashboards.<br>• **Data ingestion & modeling** – Collecting workload metadata, performance metrics, and cost data.<br>• **Partner management** – Maintaining integrations and joint go‑to‑market programs.<br>• **Customer enablement** – Workshops, certification programs, and support.<br>• **Continuous improvement** – Feedback loops from usage analytics and customer success. |
| **Key Partners** | • **Cloud providers** – AWS, Azure, GCP for API access, marketplace listings, and joint marketing.<br>• **Consulting firms** – Migration specialists who can resell or embed cloud‑migrator in their service bundles.<br>• **Compliance auditors** – To certify that the tool meets industry standards.<br>• **Open‑source communities** – Contributors to IaC templates and migration patterns.<br>• **Data vendors** – For up‑to‑date pricing and feature catalogs of cloud services. |
