# Customer Journey Map

## Aware
**Trigger Event:** Enterprise IT teams recognize the need to migrate to the cloud to meet business objectives such as cost reduction, scalability, or digital transformation.
**Friction Points:**
- Lack of awareness about the complexity of cloud migration.
- Overwhelming amount of information and tools available.
**User Emotions:** Overwhelmed, uncertain, and anxious.
**Opportunities to Delight:**
- Provide educational content (blogs, webinars) that simplifies cloud migration concepts.
- Offer case studies and success stories from similar enterprises.
**Metric:** Number of unique visitors to educational content.

## Consider
**Trigger Event:** IT teams start researching cloud migration tools and strategies.
**Friction Points:**
- Difficulty in comparing different tools and their features.
- Concerns about vendor lock-in and long-term costs.
**User Emotions:** Confused, skeptical, and cautious.
**Opportunities to Delight:**
- Offer a detailed comparison guide of cloud migration tools.
- Provide a free, no-obligation consultation to discuss specific needs.
**Metric:** Number of downloads of the comparison guide and consultation sign-ups.

## Try
**Trigger Event:** IT teams decide to test a cloud migration tool.
**Friction Points:**
- Complex setup process.
- Limited trial period or restricted features in the trial version.
**User Emotions:** Frustrated, impatient, and hopeful.
**Opportunities to Delight:**
- Offer a seamless onboarding process with step-by-step guides.
- Provide a full-featured trial with extended duration.
**Metric:** Number of trial sign-ups and completion rate of the onboarding process.

## Adopt
**Trigger Event:** IT teams decide to purchase and implement the cloud migration tool.
**Friction Points:**
- High upfront costs.
- Integration challenges with existing systems.
**User Emotions:** Excited, anxious, and hopeful.
**Opportunities to Delight:**
- Offer flexible pricing models (e.g., subscription-based).
- Provide dedicated support for integration and implementation.
**Metric:** Number of paid subscriptions and customer satisfaction scores post-implementation.

## Expand
**Trigger Event:** Enterprises see initial success and consider scaling the use of the cloud migration tool.
**Friction Points:**
- Resistance to change from other departments.
- Need for additional features to support larger-scale migrations.
**User Emotions:** Confident, ambitious, and optimistic.
**Opportunities to Delight:**
- Offer advanced training and certification programs.
- Provide custom development services for additional features.
**Metric:** Number of additional modules or services purchased and customer retention rate.
